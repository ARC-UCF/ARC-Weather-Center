## ...Discussion...

El Nino has officially reached very strong status, with a +2 degree celsius above normal temperature indicated across the Nino3.4 region. It is seeming more likely that an enhanced severe weather season across the southeast and Florida peninsula is possible in assosciation with the intense strength of this system. Minor dampening effects to the jet stream will likely provide little aide in suppressing this activity, especially as the PDO turns from negative to neutral or weakly positive. Most models indicate this occurring. 

Nor'easters are expected to pose a hazard to outdoor operations through this fall and winter in association with the shifting jet stream. These nor'easters can become capable of hurricane force wind gusts, heavy downpours, and can cause widespread power outages. Should one occur in alignment with an intended operational window, it is likely the operation must be moved indoors, or, cancelled altogether due to the effects of a nor'easter. 

Further, the active jet stream will give way to a more cloudy and wet season across the Florida peninsula. Florida is highly likely to see above average rainfall amounts across the fall and winter months, in relation to the developing El Nino. 

Climatologically speaking, El Nino conditions enhance severe weather activity, especially tornadoes, across the Florida peninsula. In El Nino falls and winters, over the combined period, Florida experiences double the usual amount of tornadoes it does in a given year, from approximately 30, doubling to 60 with the occurrence of an El Nino. However, this impact is not guaranteed, as severity of severe weather events and their occurrence relies heavily on the alignment of other systems which cannot be predicted as far in the future, such as the NAO and MJO, which will cause their own effects with their occurrence. 

It is seeming more likely that there will be fewer days this season which are favorable for outdoor operation, whether that is due to precipitation, severe weather, or wind hazards. It is also important to note that fog can start becoming problematic to operations as the fall and winter progresses, particularly during the night hours.
